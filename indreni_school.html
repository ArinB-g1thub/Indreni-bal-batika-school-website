About Us

A Rainbow of Learning & Growth

At Indreni Bal Batika, we believe every child is unique — full of colour, curiosity, and potential. Our school provides a warm, stimulating environment where children from Playgroup through Grade 3 learn to explore, create, and grow with confidence.

Located in Jagriti Marg, Maharajgunj, we are committed to holistic early childhood education — blending academic foundations with play-based learning, creative expression, and strong values.

Admissions

Join the Indreni Family

Getting your child enrolled is simple and straightforward. We welcome all families warmly.

01
Pick Up Admission Form
Visit our school in Jagriti Marg, Maharajgunj and collect the admission form from our front desk. You can also request it via email or phone.
02
Fill In the Details
Complete the form with your child's information, parent/guardian details, and desired program (Playgroup, Nursery, LKG, UKG, or Grade).
03
Submit & Confirm
Submit the completed form along with required documents. Our team will confirm your child's admission and guide you through the next steps.
